How to choose the best family lawyer?

Ask how the attorney would approach the type of issue you describe, who would handle the work, and how updates would reach you. Compare the clarity of the answers and the proposed scope. Choose based on relevant fit and understandable terms, rather than a promise about how the matter will end.

Use the same core questions in each conversation so your comparisons are useful:

  • Who will be my main contact after hiring?
  • How should I submit questions between appointments?
  • What work does the proposed fee agreement cover?
  • What information is needed to assess my situation?
  • How will I learn about decisions that require my input?

Listen for explanations you can follow. If an answer feels vague, ask for an example of how that process would work. You're deciding whether the communication style fits your needs, too.
